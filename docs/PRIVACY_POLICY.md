## What Oversea does
Oversea is a VPN client. You add a configuration (for example a link or a locked file
from a channel), and the app routes your device's internet traffic through the server
described in it, using Android's VPN service. Traffic between your device and that server
is encrypted by the protocol in the configuration.

## What we collect
Oversea has no accounts and no analytics. **We do not collect, log, store or sell your
browsing activity, traffic content, IP address or DNS queries.** Your saved configurations
and settings stay on your device (configurations in Android Keystore–backed storage) and are
not uploaded.

## The server you connect to
The VPN server is operated by whoever created the configuration, not by Oversea. That
operator can see the traffic that leaves the tunnel. Only connect to operators you trust.

## Advertising
The app shows ads using Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect your advertising ID
and device information to serve and measure ads and, where you consent, to personalise them.
Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent first, and you can change
it any time under *Settings → Ad privacy options*. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Messages inside configurations
A locked configuration can include a message and a Telegram link from its owner. These are
shown to you labelled "Sponsored" before you connect. Oversea does not control that content.

## Permissions
* **VPN service** — to create the tunnel.
* **Notifications / foreground service** — to show that the VPN is running and let you disconnect.
* **Internet / network state** — to connect and show ads.

## Exit IP display
While connected, the app asks a public IP-lookup service (ipwho.is) what address your traffic
appears to come from, so it can show you the exit IP and country. The request goes through the
tunnel, so the service sees the server's address, not yours. Nothing is stored by the app.
